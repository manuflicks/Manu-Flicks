1. What I collect

The booking form on this website does not save or send anything by itself. When you press a send button, it opens your own email or messages app with your request filled in. I only receive your information if you send that message. A booking request can include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address or phone number, from the message you send
  • The athlete's name and jersey number (Premium and Elite plans)
  • The sport, date and location of the game
  • Any note you add

I also receive anything you send me directly by email, text or phone.

2. How I use it

  • To reply to your request and confirm whether I can cover your game
  • To find the right athlete and be at the right place and time
  • To arrange payment and deliver your photos and videos
  • To answer questions you send me

I don't sell your information, and I don't use it for advertising.

3. Photos and videos of athletes

The photos and videos I take at games show athletes, coaches and fans, and many of them are minors. I use them to deliver your booking. I may also show some of them on this website or my Instagram (@manus.visualz) as examples of my work.

If you, or your child, appear in a photo on this website or my social media and want it removed, email manueligbarroso@gmail.com with the photo and where you saw it. I'll take it down. When you book, you can also ask me not to post your photos anywhere.

4. Who else sees it

I don't share your information with anyone except the services needed to run this website and deliver your booking:

  • Email and messaging: requests sent by email go through Google (Gmail). Requests sent by text go through your phone carrier and mine.
  • Website hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically log technical details, such as your IP address, when you visit a page.
  • Fonts: this website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Instagram: this website links to my Instagram. If you follow that link, Instagram's own privacy policy applies there.
  • Photo delivery: if I deliver your photos through an online gallery or file-sharing service, that service stores the files and its own privacy policy applies.

I may also share information if the law requires it.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

6. How long I keep it

I keep booking messages for as long as I need them to cover your game, deliver your photos and keep basic records. You can ask me to delete them at any time, unless I still need them to finish a booking you've already made.

7. Your choices

You can email me at any time to:

  • Ask what information I have about you
  • Correct it
  • Ask me to delete it
  • Ask me to remove a photo of you or your child from this website or my social media

8. Children

This website is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to make the booking for you. If I learn that a child under 13 has sent me personal information without a parent's permission, I'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
