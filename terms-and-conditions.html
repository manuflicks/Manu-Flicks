1. Booking

  • Send your request at least 1 day before the game.
  • A request is not a booking. Your booking is confirmed only when I reply to confirm it.
  • I may turn down a request, for example if I'm already booked or the game is too far away.

2. Plans and prices

Prices are per booking. They are not a subscription.

  • Basic, $40: 1 game or event, 20 to 30 edited photos, action shots, an online gallery and social-media-ready files.
  • Premium, $60: coverage focused on 1 athlete, 30 to 40 edited photos, action and portrait shots, 1 highlight video (30 seconds) and social-media-ready delivery.
  • Elite, $160: coverage across multiple games of a tournament, 75 to 100+ photos focused on 1 athlete (key moments, celebrations, sideline and playing shots), 1 full tournament highlight video and priority editing and delivery.

Photo counts are ranges. The exact number depends on the game, including how much the athlete plays.

3. Payment

No payment is taken on this website. When I confirm your booking, I'll tell you the price, how to pay and when payment is due.

4. Cancelling or changing a booking

  • If you need to cancel or change your booking, tell me as soon as you can.
  • If the game is cancelled or postponed, for example because of weather, contact me and we'll agree on a new date or how to handle any payment you've made.
  • If I can't make a game I've confirmed, I'll tell you as soon as possible and refund anything you've paid for that booking.

5. At the game

Schools, leagues and venues set their own rules about where photographers can stand. My shots depend on the access I'm given.

6. Delivery

I deliver edited photos and videos as digital files or through an online gallery. I'll tell you when to expect them when I confirm your booking. Elite bookings get priority editing.

7. Using your photos and videos

I keep the copyright to the photos and videos I take. When your booking is paid, you can:

  • Post them on your personal social media
  • Use them on recruiting profiles and send them to coaches
  • Print them for personal use

Please ask me in writing first if you want to sell them, use them in paid advertising or use them for a business.

8. My use of the photos

I may show photos and videos from your booking on this website and my social media as examples of my work. If you don't want that, tell me when you book or email me later, and I'll take them down. The Privacy Policy explains this in more detail.

9. Limits of responsibility

I'll do my best to capture every booking well. If something goes wrong on my side, my total responsibility for a booking is limited to the amount you paid for that booking.
